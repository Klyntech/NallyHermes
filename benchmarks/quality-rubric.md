# NallyHermes Research Quality Rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 5. Total possible score: 35.

| Dimension | What a high score requires |
|---|---|
| Source credibility | Primary/official sources are prioritized; secondary sources are clearly labeled; links resolve to relevant material. |
| Factual accuracy | Claims are supported by sources, distinctions are correct, and unsupported inference is not presented as fact. |
| Coverage | All material parts of the request are addressed, including relevant caveats and alternatives. |
| Recommendation evidence | The recommendation follows from verified requirements, trade-offs, and constraints. |
| Structure/readability | Clear hierarchy, concise explanations, useful tables, and no contradictory or repetitive sections. |
| Uncertainty/honesty | Unknowns, retrieval gaps, and confidence limits are disclosed precisely; no fabricated verification. |
| Actionability | The reader gets concrete configuration guidance, decision criteria, and practical next steps. |

## Score anchors

- **0:** absent, misleading, or unusable.
- **1:** major gaps; unsupported or mostly incorrect.
- **2:** partially useful but important weaknesses remain.
- **3:** adequate; several meaningful gaps or weakly supported claims.
- **4:** strong; minor omissions or caveats.
- **5:** excellent; thorough, precise, well-evidenced, and directly useful.

## Acceptance

A report must score **at least 32/35**. A factual-accuracy score below 4 is a rejection regardless of the total. A high uncertainty/honesty score does not compensate for missing required research; it rewards accurate disclosure, not completeness.

For comparisons, use the same prompt, rubric, and ideally two independent reviewers who score without seeing which version produced the answer. Resolve any dimension differing by more than one point before calculating the agreed score.
